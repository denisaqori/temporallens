| D13 | **Repetition policy:** the subject is the split unit, so all 6 of a subject's repetitions follow that subject; repetitions are never divided across train/validation/test. | D14 | **G3 calibration budget:** *k* is the total number of complete active-gesture trials supplied by each held-out subject, not windows and not trials per class. A trial is one gesture class at one eligible `rerepetition` in `{1, 4}`; all windows wholly contained in a selected trial are used. The grid is `{0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 17, 20, 34}`. Trial sets are nested, class-aware, repetition-balanced, and shared across strategies. Values below 17 deliberately test transfer to calibration-unseen gestures; report seen gestures, unseen gestures, and rest separately. The headline unit is **real gesture trials saved** | Denisa (unit, per-subject scope, repetitions); Codex (operational schedule and grid) | Denisa | 2026-08-03 | Accepted — resolves the pending *k* unit/scope; spec and G3/G4 configs updated; split manifest implemented by D16; runner not yet implemented |
| D15 | **G3 strategy parity:** the curves are `population_no_adaptation` (unchanged F1 population head), `real_adaptation` (F1 head adapted on the *k* real trials), and `real_plus_synthetic_adaptation` (the same F1 initialization and head-only update using the same real trials plus subject-conditioned synthetic latent windows). For *k*>0, the two trainable strategies use identical trainable surfaces, initialization, optimizer schedule, and optimizer-step budget. At *k*=0, real adaptation is a no-op; the synthetic arm may update the head using population-conditioned synthetic windows, but that point is a diagnostic rather than a matched-treatment comparison. This amends D5 and retires the ambiguous `real_only` / `real_plus_synthetic` / `real_plus_adaptation` names | Codex | Denisa | 2026-08-03 | Accepted — spec and G3/G4 configs updated; runner not yet implemented |
| D16 | **The split is frozen as data.** `configs/splits/subject_independent_v1.yaml` holds the 8 test subjects, the 32 training subjects, 8 folds of 4 validation subjects, the D12 label columns and the D13 repetition policy, plus a SHA-256 over those fields. | D18 | **F1 epoch-selection constants:** each of the eight cross-validation folds trains for exactly 30 epochs without early stopping.
